Blind: the board hides who's in play — you only see how good each one is (Dud, Decent, Quality, Legend) until their box opens.
How it works
  • 22 boxes, each hiding a footballer — drawn at random from a big squad, so it's a different line-up every game. Pick your box — you don't know who's in it.
  • Open the others one by one. The board runs from weakest (blue) to best (red). Knock out the blues, dodge the reds.
  • After each round the Gaffer offers you a player from his own squad. Deal or No Deal?
  • Late on he may offer a trade: swap one of his players onto the board in place of one of yours.
  • Last two boxes standing: keep yours or swap.
